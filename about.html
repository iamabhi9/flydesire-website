Our Story

Founded on a Vision of Excellence

Fly Desire Overseas was established in 2018 by Anuj Kumar, a B.Tech graduate from Central University Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh (2013). With a passion for helping students realize their dreams of studying abroad, Anuj founded this consultancy with a simple mission: to provide honest, transparent, and student-focused guidance.

What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted name in overseas education consultancy. Over the years, we have successfully guided hundreds of students to prestigious universities across Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, New Zealand, and Europe.

Our approach is built on understanding each student's unique aspirations and providing personalized counselling that aligns with their academic and career goals.
